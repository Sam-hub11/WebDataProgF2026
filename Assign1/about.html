About Us

Hey fellow collectors, welcome to Carr Diecast! If you have ever stared at a 1/64 scale listing online and thought, "There is no way a pocket-sized car should cost this much," you are in the right place. I am an avid 1/64 diecast collector just like you. There is something incredible about holding a highly detailed, perfectly scaled-down piece of automotive art right in the palm of your hand. But lately, our corner of the hobby has gotten out of hand. Artificial hype, scalper markups, and manufactured scarcity are driving prices through the roof. Too many collectors are overpaying just to complete a set or grab a trending release. I got tired of watching people get ripped off for premium 1/64 models. That is exactly why I started Carr Diecast.

Our Mission

This platform is built by a 1/64 collector, for 1/64 collectors. My goal is simple: to help you find the absolute best market prices and the highest manufacturing quality without overpaying. Whether you are hunting for mainline chase pieces or high-end premium brands, here is how Carr Diecast helps you protect your wallet:

    True Value Checks:
Real talk on whether a trending 1/64 model is worth the aftermarket markup.
    Price Tracking:
Deep dives into true market values so you know when to buy and when to wait.
    Quality Inspections:
Close-up looks at paint finishes, wheel rolls, crisp tampos, and casting accuracy.
    Smart Hunting:
Practical tips to help you secure the best 1/64 pieces without draining your bank account.We shouldn't have to overpay to enjoy our passion for small-scale cars. Let’s cut through the hype, focus on the details that actually matter, and build our collections smartly. Thanks for stopping by Carr Diecast, and happy hunting!
